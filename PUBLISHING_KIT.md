## Upwork portfolio description

```text
Created a portfolio-ready AI GRC risk assessment for a fictional internal customer-support assistant using synthetic scenario assumptions only.

The assessment documents the system boundary, a 10-item risk register, qualitative likelihood and impact ratings, proposed controls, accountable owners, a NIST AI RMF mapping across Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage, control gaps, and a prioritized remediation plan.

Key finding: the fictional scenario is conditionally positioned and not pilot-ready until critical data-governance, retrieval-security, evaluation, change-management, and incident-response gaps have documented design evidence and acceptance results.

This is a desk-based educational portfolio lab. It does not use real client data, claim implemented controls, or provide legal, regulatory, or certification conclusions.
```
